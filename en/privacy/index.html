Information

Privacy / GDPR

This page explains how Foundation 432 Hertz handles supporter information submitted through the website.

Data controller

The data controller is Foundation 432 Hertz, UIC/EIK 204925949. Contact: info@432hz-foundation.com.

What data is collected

The site may collect information you choose to provide, such as name, email address, language preference, support interest, message content, and donation certificate request details.

Why data is collected

Information is used for Listening Circle communication, concerts, recordings, archive updates, campaigns, support communication, and donation certificate requests.

Consent and unsubscribe

You can unsubscribe or ask to stop receiving communication at any time by writing to info@432hz-foundation.com.

Tools and processors

CRM/form integrations are prepared but not live. When connected, the site may use form and CRM tools such as Brevo, Tally, or Google Sheets to process supporter information.

Retention

Information is kept only as long as needed for communication, support administration, documentation, or legal/accounting context. No fixed retention period is claimed here.

Rights and contact

You may contact the foundation to ask about access, correction, deletion, or restriction of personal data at info@432hz-foundation.com.

Updates to this policy

This page may be updated when form and CRM integrations are activated or when the website support flow changes.
