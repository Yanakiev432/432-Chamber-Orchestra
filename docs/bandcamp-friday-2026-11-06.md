## Proposed Bandcamp artist biography (English)

432 Chamber Orchestra is an independent string orchestra founded in Sofia, Bulgaria, in November 2013 by conductor Ivan K. Yanakiev. Its work spans classical string repertoire, chamber performances, recordings and archival documentation.

The orchestra's debut was recognised in the Bulgarian National Radio's Musician of the Year 2015 awards, in the Debut category. After a period of interruption, the orchestra is preparing the next phase of its work.

Listen to the recordings below. Purchasing an album is one way to support the orchestra's return and its future artistic activity.

432 Chamber Orchestra is the lead artistic project of Foundation 432 Hertz.

Contact: info@432hz-foundation.com
