## Fit Score Rule

Score five criteria from 1 to 5:

- Cultural fit
- Decision access
- Financial capacity
- Reputational safety
- Useful beyond money

Maximum score: 25.

- `20-25`: first priority
- `15-19`: good prospect
- `10-14`: nurture
- Under `10`: ignore for now

Keep `07 Do Not Contact` separate and respect it before any outreach.
